# Protecting dictation data

[Deutsch](../de/admin-guide/privacy.md)

A dictation can have several separate records: original audio and its hash, transcript revisions, a generated summary, workflow history, import receipts and an output ledger. Where an agent is used, its inventory, transfer and receipt information can also identify a source file. Include these stores and their backups in your local access and retention review.

Use the narrowest applicable pool or individual grants. A site administrator can have audited reading or playback access. Editing, release and original-audio export have separate controls. Use a neutral synthetic recording for acceptance, exports and support screenshots. Avoid putting dictated content into account names or operational notes. The optional [SMTP notification](email-setup.md) may carry a recording title outside the application.

A browser classified as BYOD does not gain offline audio storage or bundle export from having a server login. Managed offline storage is prepared but disabled. Voice profiles require consent for the author’s own voice; review withdrawal in the relevant profile. No device or clinical-system integration is certified solely by a synthetic test. Define retention periods and accountable operators as part of local deployment. [Archiving](retention.md) · [Audit log](audit-log.md)
