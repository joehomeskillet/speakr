# Clinic administration

[Deutsch](../de/admin-guide/index.md)

Open **Verwaltung** to reach the clinic administration panel. The landing page, **Benutzer und Rollen**, brings account details, pool membership and specific permissions together. The German labels below match the interface.

| What you need to do | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| Look up an account, change its access or resolve a pool assignment | **Konten und Pools** → [accounts](user-management.md) or [pools](group-management.md) |
| Maintain shared vocabulary, templates and local rules | **Inhalte** and **Richtlinien** |
| Inspect incoming files, processing jobs or an ASR profile | **Betrieb** |
| Inspect system settings, audit entries or choose a model | **System** → [settings](system-settings.md), [audit](audit-log.md) or [model selection](model-configuration.md) |

**Choose the correct role.** Site administration and local identity administration are separate. A pool lead coordinates work within a routed team. Releasing a revision and exporting original audio require their own permissions. An audited read or playback does not give the reader permission to change a dictation. If a page reports that the account has no access, review the relevant role or scoped grant with an authorised administrator.

For upgrades start with the [migration guide](migration-guide.md). For retention and protection of clinical information see [archiving](retention.md) and [privacy](privacy.md).
