# Clinic user guide

English · [Deutsch](../de/user-guide/index.md)

Use the browser to record a dictation, check the locally recognised text against its audio and pass it to the authorised writing service. This guide uses the **German control labels** that appear in the clinic interface.

## Choose your task

- **Author:** [Sign in and find your way](first-steps.md), then [record, save and submit](recording.md). Saving first creates a server draft. Submitting passes it into the workflow.
- **Typist or reviewer:** [Open and correct a transcript](transcripts.md), [inspect a template](transcript-templates.md) or [work with the keyboard](keyboard-shortcuts.md).
- **Working with colleagues:** [Hand over a dictation](sharing.md), [understand pools](groups.md) and [find an item in the lists](folders.md).
- **Your workstation:** [Settings](settings.md), [browser installation and offline limits](pwa.md), [mobile and device import](companion-apps.md).
- **Looking for an upstream feature?** See [Ambrose](ambrose.md), [cross-recording questions](inquire-mode.md), [API tokens](api-tokens.md) and the [API reference](api-reference.md).

A visible list item, permission to listen, an editing lease, release authority and permission to export original audio are separate. Sign in with your assigned account and act on the dictations it shows.
