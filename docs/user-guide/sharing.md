# Hand a dictation to the writing service

English · [Deutsch](../de/user-guide/sharing.md)

1. After recording or uploading, choose **Entwurf sichern**. The confirmation tells you the draft is on the server and has yet to be submitted.
2. Choose **Einreichen** once and wait for **Eingereicht**. In **Arbeitsliste**, check the row for **Schreibkräfte-Pool**, **Zugewiesen an** and **Übernommen von** where available. These identify the pool, assignment and person currently working on the dictation.
3. If the recipient cannot see it, check that it was submitted and the expected pool is shown. Ask an authorised coordinator to check assignment and rights. An editing lease prevents a second writer from overwriting an active session.

Upstream public links and ad hoc sharing are not the clinic handover mechanism. Sending the address of a details page does not grant access to its contents. A colleague who can listen may still lack the separate right to export original audio.
