# Work with writing and author pools

English · [Deutsch](../de/user-guide/groups.md)

A writing pool receives work for its members. An author pool groups dictating authors. The two serve different purposes. The **Diktiert von** column continues to show the dictating author even when another person takes over correction.

- To see who is responsible, open **Arbeitsliste** and inspect **Schreibkräfte-Pool**, **Zugewiesen an** and **Übernommen von**. A blank holder is different from a missing pool.
- If a task should be visible to your team, ask the responsible administrator to check **Verwaltung & Seiten → Schreibkräfte-Pools**, then the account under **Benutzer und Rollen**. Author associations are managed under **Diktierende und Autorenpools**.
- For a missing action, specify which action you need: view, listen, edit, review or original-audio export. Visibility of the pool alone does not establish all these permissions.

Open groups and invitations from the original Speakr guide do not enrol staff in a clinic workflow. [Handing over a dictation](sharing.md).
