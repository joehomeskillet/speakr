# Text blocks and document templates

English · [Deutsch](../de/user-guide/transcript-templates.md)

In an editable dictation, open **Textbaustein einfügen** in **Schreibplatz**. Choose an available resource and check its name, version and any insertion-marker warning. The current selector records that choice in this view. It does not insert the template body into your transcript. If you need a heading or phrase, enter the approved wording yourself, compare it with the audio and save.

An authorised administrator can open **Verwaltung & Seiten → Sprachbefehle und Vorlagen → Vorlagen** to inspect centrally saved template versions, assignments and insertion-marker previews. A marker warning in the preview needs attention before the template is used. Contact the person responsible for your pool if an expected resource is missing.

The original Speakr *transcript templates* are a formatting language for transcript exports. Central clinic document templates have their own versions and assignments. The current clinic DOCX output takes the released transcript and does not offer a choice of a stored template or bookmark.
